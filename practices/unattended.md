# Unattended session: these replace any step that waits for a person

- An issue with a *Done when* list is your approval: start without asking. Without that list, hand off and ask for one. {core; initial}
- Never wait for an answer. When something needs the owner (a dependency the issue does not name, a choice it does not settle, a failure outside its scope), hand off and end. {core; initial}
- A handoff is a comment on the issue or PR, under 30 lines: what is done, what is left, the decision needed, the head commit and the next command. {core; initial}
- Commit with a Conventional Commit message and push your branch before you hand off or end. {core; initial}
